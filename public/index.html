Origin

From Ghana,
to your table

Shito is a black pepper sauce from Ghana. It sits on the table at every meal: on rice, with kenkey and fried fish, in a sandwich, on an egg.

At Mum's Delice, every jar is made by hand in Edmonton, Alberta.

  1. In GhanaA table sauce found in every kitchen.
  2. HandmadeEvery jar is made by hand, with simple ingredients.
  3. In EdmontonFor the diaspora, and for everyone who likes heat with flavour.
Smoked anchovies (Doèvi)Smoked West African anchovies, known as Doèvi or Davi, give dark Ghanaian shito its deep, savoury base.
